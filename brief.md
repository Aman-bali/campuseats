CampusEats Brief

What

CampusEats is a campus food ordering system. It allows students to view available food options, select food items, place orders, make payments, and track their orders. Restaurants can manage their menus and orders, while administrators can manage users, restaurants, and the overall system.

The goal of CampusEats is to make food ordering on campus easier and more organized for students and food providers.

 Who

The main users of CampusEats are:

1. Students — browse food options, place orders, make payments, and track orders.
2. Restaurants/Food Vendors— manage menus, receive orders, and update order status.
3. Administrators — manage users, vendors, and system information.

Main Users and Actions

 Student

A student can:

* Register and log in.
* Browse restaurants and food items.
* Search for food.
* Add food items to a cart.
* Place an order.
* Make a payment.
* Track an order.
* Cancel an order when permitted.

Restaurant/Food Vendor

A restaurant can:

* Log in.
* Add food items.
* Update food item information.
* Remove food items.
* View incoming orders.
* Accept or reject orders.
* Update order status.

Administrator

An administrator can:

* Manage users.
* Manage restaurants.
* Manage food items and system information.
* Monitor orders.
* Manage the overall CampusEats system.

System Goal

CampusEats connects students with campus food vendors through a single ordering system. It provides a structured way to browse food, place orders, process payments, and track order progress.
